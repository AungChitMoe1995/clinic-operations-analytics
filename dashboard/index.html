💡 How to Read Operational Metrics (Clinical Evaluator & HR Guide) Standard Healthcare KPI Definitions
Median Wait (P50)
Typical Patient Experience: Exactly half of all patients waited less than this, and half waited more. Unlike a mathematical average, the median is not distorted by rare extreme outliers.
P90 Wait Time (90th Percentile)
Worst 10% Longest Waits: The threshold separating the fastest 90% from the slowest 10%. In healthcare (NHS, Australia, Singapore MOH), P90 measures severe backlogs that trigger walkouts and formal complaints.
Slot Overrun Rate (%)
Consultation Exceeding Slot: The percentage of consults exceeding scheduled appointment duration by >5 minutes, identifying clinician workload strain and appointment template mismatch.

📋 Clinical & Operational Findings Summary

1. Mid-Morning Queuing Cascades Driven by Unbuffered Slot Compression
Evidence: Morning P90 wait exploded from 41.9 min (Pre) to 76.3 min (Post). Meanwhile, afternoon clinic waiting times remained completely stable (35.2m vs 35.8m).
Clinical Cause: Compressing morning slots to 15 minutes removed schedule buffer times. Stochastic overruns compounded across consecutive patients by 10:30 AM.
2. Case-Mix Mismatch for Complex Chronic Reviews
Evidence: Dr. Arthur Vance (Internal Medicine) had a 48.3% slot overrun rate and delivered 1,307 clinical hours. Chronic Care Plan reviews took 24.5 min on average.
Clinical Cause: Booking multi-morbid patients into shortened morning slots without protected duration triggered schedule slippage.
3. Actionable Operational Recommendation: Catch-Up Buffers
Recommendation: Insert an unbooked 15-minute administrative buffer slot at 10:30 AM on all morning clinician templates. In queuing theory, this absorbs upstream overruns and prevents queue collapse before the 11:00 AM hour.
4. Actionable Recommendation: Dynamic Nursing Intake
Recommendation: Reallocate triage nursing shifts to open a 3rd triage intake station between 08:30 and 10:15 AM, eliminating the front-end vitals bottleneck where lobby wait exceeded 18 minutes.
